# Paintings used by the Vishnu Sahasranama page

These four devotional paintings were supplied by the site's owner as the
imagery for the page, which turns them into moving brush-stroke particles.
They are other artists' work and are not covered by this repository's licence.
The artists were not named where the images were found; if you know who made
one, add their name here, and ask their permission before publishing the site
publicly.

| file | subject | artist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `many-faces.webp` | the many-faced blue Vishvarupa, Krishna with his flute within | unknown |
| `lingodbhava.webp` | the blue Lord in a burning sky over Brahma and Vishnu, with a lotus | unknown |
| `vishvarupa-arjuna.webp` | the Universal Form towering over Arjuna | unknown |
| `trimurti.webp` | the three faces of the Trimurti | unknown |

`many-faces.webp` is cropped by 8 px at the top and `lingodbhava.webp` has its
grey frame cropped away; otherwise they are as supplied.
