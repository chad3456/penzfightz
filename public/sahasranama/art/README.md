# Paintings used by the Vishnu Sahasranama page

These four images were supplied by the site's owner, who reports that they are
public-domain photographs of devotional paintings. The page turns them into
moving brush-stroke particles.

| file | subject |
| --- | --- |
| `many-faces.webp` | the many-faced blue Vishvarupa, Krishna with his flute within |
| `lingodbhava.webp` | the blue Lord in a burning sky over Brahma and Vishnu, with a lotus |
| `vishvarupa-arjuna.webp` | the Universal Form towering over Arjuna |
| `trimurti.webp` | the three faces of the Trimurti |

`many-faces.webp` is cropped by 8 px at the top and `lingodbhava.webp` has its
grey frame cropped away; otherwise they are as supplied.
