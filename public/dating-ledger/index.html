The rise

From a niche to the main way people meet

The share of Americans who had tried online dating roughly tripled in under a decade, then levelled off. Among couples who met in 2017, online was the leading way to meet.

        The fall

        Fewer people paying, and the first drop in users

        The decline shows up first in money: fewer people paying for Tinder and Bumble. In the UK, Ofcom recorded the first fall in use of the biggest apps, while the total number of adults visiting any dating service stayed broadly flat.

                Culture contrasts

                The same forces land differently in each country

                Apps now match the office as Japan’s top way to meet a spouse, while in India most women still do not choose their husband themselves. Marriages are falling in China and recovering in South Korea. Young men and women report very different love lives.
