# Ghar 624

A room-by-room 3D interior designer for a 624 sq ft 2 BHK: the entrance opens
into a kitchen with two black granite ottas, then a small living room, then two
master bedrooms of about 12′ × 13′ with attached baths. Published as a claude.ai
artifact; `ghar-624.html` is its source (page content only — the artifact
platform adds the document skeleton).

- **Rooms**: Plan, Kitchen, Living, Bedroom 1, Bedroom 2 — each room's size and
  ceiling height can be set in feet; views are eye level (look around), ceiling,
  dollhouse and top; Evening turns on the cove LEDs, lamps and diyas; the fan spins.
- **Choices**: POP false ceilings (cove, double cove, round centre, wooden slats,
  linear lights, coffered) with LED colour; fans in six styles and colours; 12
  paints and a feature wall in 15 wallpapers, textures and panelling; 7 floors;
  6 TV units; 5 bookshelves; sofa, rug and curtains; portraits in 8 artworks or
  your own uploaded photo, as single, pair, trio or gallery wall; 5 wardrobes with
  shutter and accent finishes; 4 headboards; a mandir in 4 styles for any room;
  kitchen layout, granite, cabinets and backsplash.
- **Saving**: designs are kept in the artifact's database so they open on every
  device (and for anyone the page is shared with); the current draft is kept in
  the browser; pictures of the 3D view save as PNG.
- **Estimate**: a rough ₹ range per room and for the whole home, for planning only.

Everything is drawn in code with three.js (0.185.1, from jsDelivr): the furniture
is built from simple solids and every texture is painted on a canvas.
