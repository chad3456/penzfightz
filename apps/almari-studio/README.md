# Almari Studio

A wardrobe designer for a master bedroom. Size the wardrobe to the wall,
lay out the inside bay by bay, choose shutters, finishes and handles, and get a
cut list and a rough estimate. Published as a claude.ai artifact;
`almari-studio.html` is its source (page content only — the artifact platform
adds the document skeleton).

- **Size**: width, main height, loft, depth, skirting, board thickness and
  type, in mm, cm or feet and inches. Quick starts from 6 ft to 12 ft. Checks
  for the ceiling, the wall, wide shutters, sheet height, hanging depth,
  overfull bays, wide shelves, drawers out of reach and swing room past the bed.
- **Inside**: any number of bays, each with one or two shutters and a stack of
  fittings from the floor up: long and short hanging, shelves, inner and outer
  drawers, jewellery drawer, tie & belt, trouser, shoe and saree pull-outs,
  wire basket, locker, open display, handbag cubbies and a pull-down hanger.
  Nine bay layouts to start from, LED strips in four colour temperatures.
- **Shutters**: hinged or sliding (2–4 panels); flat, shaker, fluted, fine
  ribbed, V-groove, louvred, aluminium frame, glass, mirror, cane and arched;
  seven finish layouts (one finish, alternate doors, centre accent, end
  accents, contrast loft, top band, contrast frame); a different profile per
  bay; any single door painted on its own.
- **Finishes**: 2,028 finishes in 12 collections, every one painted in code
  (woodgrains in 28 species × 7 tones × 3 cuts, veneers, solid matte,
  high-gloss acrylic, super matte, PU lacquer, stone and marble, concrete and
  terrazzo, metals, fabric/cane/leather looks, printed patterns, glass and
  mirrors), each with its own code, colour (hex), colour family and surface.
  Search, filter by collection, style edit, colour family and surface; 24
  colour combinations; suggestions that go with the chosen finish; favourites
  and recents; photos of real samples can be added as "My swatches".
- **Views**: 3D room (doors open and close, day and evening light), front
  elevation and inside layout, both drawn to scale with dimensions.
- **Estimate**: board and finish sheets, edge banding, hinges, runners,
  accessories, handles, LED and labour from the design, at editable example
  rates; full cut list as CSV.
- **Saving**: designs save to the artifact's database (or this browser), plus
  PNG pictures, CSV, a JSON design file and a text summary for the carpenter.

Finish names and codes are this studio's own and do not belong to any brand;
match a supplier's shade card by eye or hex, or photograph the sample.
Everything is drawn with three.js (0.185.1, from jsDelivr).
